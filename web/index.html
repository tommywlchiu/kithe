Kithe

A puppy that grows when you move.

Kithe is a cozy dog-raising game for iPhone and Android. Your real-life steps keep your pup fed, happy, and growing. Money can buy outfits, never care.

How it works

  1. Move.

    Walk, run, or roll. Kithe reads your steps from Apple Health or Health Connect and turns them into Pawprints.

  2. Care.

    Spend Pawprints on meals, brushing, toys, and training. A few minutes a day is plenty.

  3. Bond.

    Your pup grows from a wobbly puppy into your best friend, learns tricks, and writes a diary about your days together.

Your goal adapts to you, so a walk around the block can fill your pup's bowl just like a long run.

Why Kithe is different

Care can't be bought.

Food and care come only from moving. Outfits and decor are optional extras that never change how your pup grows.

A dog who notices.

Your pup keeps a diary about your days together, and remembers the rainy walk from last month.

Real dog life.

Teething, zoomies, stolen socks, a first vet visit, muddy paws after a walk. The everyday moments of owning a dog, minus the vet bills.

No guilt, ever.

Your pup never dies and never starves. Sick, injured, or traveling? Rest Mode tucks them in for a cozy nap until you're back.

Made to be calm.

Soft kibble crunches, sleepy snores, and rain on the window. Designed to be the most relaxing two minutes of your day.

FAQ

When can I play?

Beta invites go out in small waves once the first build is ready. Waitlist members get in first.

Is it free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play. An optional membership and cosmetic outfits come later, and neither can buy care for your pup.

iPhone or Android?

Both.

What data does Kithe use?

Step, exercise, and wheelchair-push counts from Apple Health or Health Connect. Your raw health data stays on your phone; only your daily goal progress is saved to your account. No ads, and your data is never sold.

Is the diary written by AI?

Yes. Your pup's diary is written with help from AI, based on a simple summary of your week, like how many days you hit your goal. You can turn it off, and the game works fully without it.

Can my pup die?

Never. The worst that happens is a mopey pup, and one good day cheers them right up.

What if I can't walk much?

Your goal adapts to your normal activity, wheelchair pushes count the same as steps, and Rest Mode pauses everything when you're sick, injured, or traveling.

What does "Kithe" mean?

It rhymes with "lithe." It's an old Scots word meaning "to make known," fitting for a pup who tells you about your days together.
