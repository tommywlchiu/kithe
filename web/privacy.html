Privacy

The beta waitlist

When you join the waitlist we collect:

  • your email address, to send your beta invite and beta updates;
  • your phone type (iPhone or Android), to send the right kind of invite;
  • your confirmation that you are 18 or older, because the beta is for adults;
  • if you answer it, why you don't have a dog right now, to understand who Kithe is for;
  • which link brought you here (for example a social post), to know which posts work.

We only email you about Kithe. We never sell your information or use it for advertising. The waitlist is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in the United States.

Being removed

Reply "remove me" to any Kithe email, or write to marqueofficialhq@gmail.com, and we will delete your waitlist entry and stop emailing you.

Health data in the Kithe app

The Kithe app reads your step, exercise, and wheelchair-push counts from Apple Health or Health Connect, only with your permission, to calculate progress toward your daily movement goal and care for your pup. Manually typed entries are left out so progress reflects real movement.

  • Raw health records stay on your phone.
  • Only your daily goal progress is used in the game.
  • Health data is never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with third parties.
  • You can turn off access at any time in the Health app or Health Connect settings.

The app never shows or asks for calories, weight, or body measurements.
